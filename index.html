Here is a description of every Dream Team at COOL Church.

Buddies
Empowering children ages 3-8 with unique needs through worship, Biblical teaching, and personal interaction.
Cool Beans
Creating a warm, welcoming first impression through great coffee, hospitality, and heart. One cup at a time, this team sets the tone for community, conversation, and connection.
Culture
Supporting next steps, salvation decisions, baptisms, church connection, and the monthly Culture Class Fast Track.
Data
Supporting all Dream Teams through data entry, Kids check-in, event registration, and detail-oriented administration.
Experience
Creating a five-star experience by welcoming guests, bringing the energy, and setting the vibe in every space.
Health
Medical team on standby for any health emergencies or accidents during services and events.
Home & Fam
Building brand presence, creating spaces, and caring for the Dream Team through hospitality, refreshments, birthday cards, and celebration events.
Host
Ensuring everyone finds their place — assisting with seating, facilitating the offering, and maintaining a distraction-free service environment.
Kids
Investing in children ages 6 months to 5th grade through worship, Biblical teaching, small groups, and personal interaction.
Musicians
Playing alongside the worship team to create a powerful musical worship experience. Audition and training required.
Moves
Creative leaders using the gift of dance to uplift stage productions, events, and Sunday service specials.
Parking
Serving as the first impression — welcoming people on and off the property and ensuring a safe parking experience.
Prayer
Interceding daily for prayer requests, leading Saturday morning prayer, and supporting salvation calls on Sundays.
Production
Managing lights, cameras, visual elements, and the COOL Church livestream that connects the church worldwide.
Security
Creating and sustaining safe environments in the auditorium, lobbies, and kids center for all services and events.
Spanish Translation
Translating service and sermon notes for Spanish-speaking congregation members.
Storytellers
Producing content, photographing services, managing social media, and shaping the COOL Church visual identity.
Worship
Facilitating a powerful worship experience through vocals, instruments, and audio engineering. Evaluation and training required.
Youth
Investing in middle and high schoolers through Biblical teaching, small groups, life lessons, and personal interaction.
Communicators
Communicating announcements to the congregation each Sunday. Must be dynamic and able to draw people in.
Virtual Experience
Hosting online chats, praying for the virtual family, and creating an engaging online campus experience.
Cares
Organizes all outreach and volunteering opportunities outside the church walls. Note: Cares is not a regular Sunday serve team — this team supports the church's local outreach efforts and spearheads major events like Serve Day every summer and Easter Egg Bowl each year.
